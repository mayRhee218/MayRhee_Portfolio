THE IDEA

A little about the project.

Participated in Hongik University’s summer visual-design workshop, Invisible Seoul, presenting a work titled Gangnam (South of the Han River).

View the original Wix portfolio ↗

DESIGN NOTES / 01

Visual concept

Layered a contemporary Gangnam map over a drawing of the old district. The contrast between irregular historic roads and today’s orthogonal street grid informed the treatment of the typography.

DESIGN NOTES / 02

Research and narrative

Placed text about the district’s development, including housing, education, entertainment and hospitals, at relevant locations on the map.

DESIGN NOTES / 03

Historical context

Used text on a translucent layer to describe the political and urban history behind Gangnam’s rapid development.
