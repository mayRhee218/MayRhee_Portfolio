THE IDEA

A little about the project.

A sequence of three themed goods-design exercises developed across several weeks.

View the original Wix portfolio ↗

DESIGN NOTES / 01

Week 1 · Pin button

Designed three badge variations commemorating the May 18 Gwangju Democratization Movement, using memorial imagery, a bloodstained hand and a chrysanthemum to honour those who died defending democracy.

DESIGN NOTES / 02

Week 2 · Tarot cards

Created an original tarot card inspired by medieval settings and assassination, using Illustrator.

DESIGN NOTES / 03

Week 3 · Postage stamp

Designed a commemorative stamp based on NU’EST’s song Back to Me (Parallel Universe), using melting clocks to express fractured time and lovers becoming entwined.
